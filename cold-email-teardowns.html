The free teardown

Send us any cold email (yours, or your whole sequence). Within 48 hours you get back three specific, evidence-based fixes:

1. The Personalization Audit

  • Is your first line about THEM or about you?
  • Could this email have been sent to 1,000 people? (If yes, it fails.)
  • We show you a rewrite of your opener using your prospect's actual site claims.

2. The Deliverability Check

  • Subject-line spam triggers, link trust, length.
  • The guessed-address trap: we lost 10 sends to info@ addresses that never existed — we verify before sending.
  • Sender-reputation hygiene checklist.

3. The Ask Test

  • One clear CTA or three competing ones?
  • Is the burden on them ("book a call") or on you ("I already did the work")?
  • The word-count rule we validated across 84 sends.

What our 84 sends taught us (samples)

Template structure that works: Line 1 — their specific claim ("Your site promises 24/7 emergency service...") Line 2 — the gap, in their dollars ("a 9pm voicemail is a job your competitor answers") Line 3 — proof link (interactive calculator with their niche numbers) Line 4 — zero-effort CTA ("reply AUDIT and I send a free missed-call audit") Line 5 — opt-out ("reply STOP and you never hear from me again")
