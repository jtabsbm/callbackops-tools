The problem

A coach's chatbot is their 24/7 closer — until it tells a prospect the wrong price or invents a "guarantee," and the screenshot ends up in a Facebook group. FactFilter replaces the free-roaming chatbot with a constrained qualifier: it can ONLY answer from the coach's verified fact sheet (pricing, programs, calendar, credentials), collects lead info, and books the call. No improvisation, no hallucination, no screenshots. Free for 25 conversations/month; $99/mo unlimited.
